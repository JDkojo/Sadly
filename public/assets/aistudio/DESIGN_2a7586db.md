## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the vitality, freshness, and dependability of an artisan, farm-to-table grocer translated into a high-speed digital commerce experience. The aesthetic balances crisp agricultural authenticity with the frictionless utility of on-demand logistics.

### Personality & Values
- **Vital & Crisp:** Clear, energetic visuals that make produce look picked moments ago.
- **Transparent & Trustworthy:** Honest pricing, prominent nutritional cues, clear origin tags, and clean borders that leave no ambiguity.
- **Effortless Velocity:** Low-friction touch targets, rapid quantity adjustments, and visual clarity designed for glanceability during quick shopping routines.

### Visual Style
A blend of **Modern Clean Commerce** and **Organic Tactility**. The interface relies on luminous off-white paper tones, vibrant botanical emerald greens, zesty citrus alerts, structured micro-borders, and tactile pill-shaped touchpoints. Surfaces remain predominantly flat or subtly elevated via soft organic under-shadows, ensuring fast scan speeds without visual fatigue.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical chlorophyls, warm sunburst citrus, and clean garden neutrals. High contrast ensures readability in outdoor daylight and fast mobile interactions.

### Key Roles
- **Primary (`#15803D`):** The signature deep garden emerald. Used for key actions, brand anchors, primary CTA states, active navigation indicators, and verified organic emblems.
- **Secondary (`#F97316`):** Vibrant sunburst orange. Reserved for urgency, promotional tags, flash sale ribbons, notifications, and dynamic discount badges.
- **Tertiary (`#16A34A`):** Vivid leaf green. Used for positive status chips (e.g., "In Stock", "Ripe Today"), quantity increment accents, and secondary visual confirmation.
- **Neutral Core (`#1A2E1A`):** Deep botanical charcoal. Replaces harsh pure black for primary typography and crisp structural borders, maintaining organic warmth.

### Functional Surfaces & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base:** Soft herbal tint `#F8FAF6` creates an ambient, glare-free background.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Sheet):** `#FFFFFF` for pristine contrast behind product photography.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Troughs/Inputs):** `#F1F5F0` for text field wells, quantity steppers, and pill-tab tracks.
- **Border / Divider:** Crisp natural edge `#E2E8DF` for delicate 1px structural framing.

## Typography

The design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across display, body, and label roles. Its geometric roundness reinforces the approachable nature of local food and wellness, while its clean counters maintain structural clarity under dense data constraints.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (`700` and `800`) with tight letter spacing for impactful merchandising, category hero banners, and express checkout summaries.
- **Body:** Open and legible (`400`) with generous line-heights to support effortless scanning of ingredient lists, sourcing origins, and delivery notes.
- **Labels:** Compact, crisp weights (`600` and `700`) styled with subtle tracking for badge text, pill tabs, weight specifications (e.g., `500g`, `1 lb`), and pricing currencies.

## Layout & Spacing

A compact, touch-optimized fluid layout built specifically for continuous vertical scrolling, rapid catalog navigation, and bottom-sheet checkouts.

### Grid & Density Rules
- **Mobile Canvas (<640px):** Single or dual-column product grid with a 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margin and 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters between product cards to balance thumb target size and visual density.
- **Tablet / Large Handheld (640px - 1024px):** 4-column responsive grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) outer canvas margins and 16px (`1rem`) gutters.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built strictly around increments of 4px and 8px. Use `space-sm` (8px) for tightly grouped content (e.g., price + unit measure), `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, and `space-xl` (32px) between major merchandising modules.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid visual murkiness and highlight the bright, clean natural color palette, depth is achieved primarily through **crisp low-contrast outlines** paired with **tonal surface stacking** and **subtle ambient contact shadows**.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAF6` base with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Product Cards & List Tiles):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed in a 1px solid `#E2E8DF` boundary. Features a gentle, diffused ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(26, 46, 26, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Pill Navigation & Floating Add Actions):** Solid white or emerald fills with a slightly lifted shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Bar, Floating Cart & Bottom Sheets):** Elevated backdrop layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and tinted borders: `0 -4px 20px rgba(26, 46, 26, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometric language reflects organic softness within an engineered, modern frame.

### Radius Distribution
- **Level 2 Standard (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for micro-badges, input fields, and standard list containers.
- **Level 2 Large (`1rem` / `16px`):** Used for product cards, banner modules, and bottom modal sheets.
- **Pill Shape (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for high-intent interactive components: CTA buttons, quantity stepper housings, tag chips, and filter toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Fully rounded pill (`rounded-full`), `#15803D` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), height 48px on mobile. Active feedback: subtle scale transform (`0.98`) and background shift to `#14532D`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Pill-shaped, `#F1F5F0` background, `#15803D` text, 1px `#E2E8DF` border.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** `#FFFFFF` background, 16px radius, 1px `#E2E8DF` border.
- **Image Trough:** Clean square aspect ratio (1:1) framed by `#F8FAF6` background to ensure irregular produce photography looks uniform.
- **Badging:** Floating top-left tags with 4px margin:
  - *Organic:* `#15803D` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-sm`, rounded-full.
  - *Discount / Promo:* `#F97316` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-sm`, rounded-full.
- **Footer Interaction:** Price anchored on left; rapid action on right.

### Rapid Add-to-Cart & Quantity Steppers
- **Idle State:** Compact pill button (`+ ADD`), 32px height, 1px `#15803D` border, `#15803D` text.
- **Active State (Quantity > 0):** Transforms into an encapsulated `#15803D` pill stepper housing a minus button, middle numerical counter (`#FFFFFF`, `label-md`), and plus button with haptic touch feedback.

### Chips & Pill Tabs
- **Category Filter Tabs:** Horizontally scrolling pill rail. Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` fill, `#E2E8DF` border, `#1A2E1A` text. Selected state: `#15803D` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, shadow token applied.

### Input Fields
- **Search Bar:** Height 44px, pill-shaped, `#F1F5F0` background, `#1A2E1A` placeholder text, zero border in rest state, transitioning to a 1.5px `#15803D` border when focused.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 20px square with 6px border radius, 1.5px `#15803D` border when unchecked. When checked: solid `#15803D` fill with white checkmark glyph.
- **Radios:** 20px round, inner 8px circle fill using `#15803D` upon selection.

### Sticky Bottom Cart Bar
- Fixed bottom mobile container with 16px blur backdrop, containing a dynamic delivery threshold indicator (progress bar using `#16A34A`), total price tally, and high-velocity checkout trigger button.
